---
title: "Collaborations"
description: "Custom stickers for communities, builders, brands, IP, and artists."
---

Alongside our regular series, we plan to make custom sticker designs for:

- Community and builder meetups.
- Events and hackathons.
- Seminars and similar gatherings.

We are also open to collaborations with other brands, projects, and artists.

These projects can take the form of a special series, with the sticker types and production format chosen for the event.
