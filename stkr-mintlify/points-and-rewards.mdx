title: "Points & rewards"
description: "Planned benefits for collectors and buyers."
---

We plan to connect points, events, and rewards with:

- Ownership of Vault stickers and the sticker NFTs issued with them.
- Purchase history across Vault, Wild, and special stickers.

Sticker purchases are planned to earn points. Details of the points system and related rewards are scheduled to be announced **during Q4 2026**.

These are plans. Point calculations and specific rewards have not yet been announced.

Our plans for using part of the project's sales revenue to buy back collaborators' assets are described in [Revenue & buybacks](/revenue-and-buybacks).
