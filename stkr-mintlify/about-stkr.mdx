.STKR is a team that makes stickers.

We make stickers based on IP from Web3, blockchain, and crypto projects. We see active builders and communities in this space, and we want to make something they can use in everyday life.

## Why stickers?

A sticker is a simple way to show what you are part of. Put one on your laptop, another device, or anywhere you choose.

We believe these everyday reminders can strengthen a sense of belonging and pride, and bring more energy to the people building and supporting a project.

For NFT-based communities, we also use snapshots to give holders the right to buy particular stickers. It is one way to make participation in a community part of the product.

We are a Web3 team too. Making stickers is how we want to contribute.
