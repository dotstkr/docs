---
title: "Sticker types"
---

Our main sticker types are **Vault** and **Wild**. Their availability and pack contents depend on the project and series.

| Type | Personalization | Packaging | Who can buy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Vault** | Personalized, with a wallet address or registered wallet name | A sealed card case | Holders of that NFT number at the snapshot who still hold it, during the sales period |
| **Wild** | Made for the whole community; NFT holders can buy the version for their own NFT number | A sticker pack without engraving or a storage case | Anyone, during the sales period, within the purchase limit |

See [Purchasing & eligibility](/buying-and-eligibility) for the full rules.

## Vault

Vault is a limited-edition, personalized sticker made for collecting.

It is sold in a sealed card case. An authentication chip identifies the limited-edition item, making it possible to verify the Vault sticker as a unique piece.

At purchase, the buyer's blockchain wallet address or a name registered through a wallet naming service is used for personalization. The product also includes a specially designed background and identifying banner. Their form and arrangement may change between series.

A **sticker NFT**, with benefits planned for later, is also issued when a Vault sticker is purchased.

## Wild

Wild is a sticker made for a project's whole community and builders, not for a single collector.

It is a regular sticker sold in packs, without the engraving or storage case included with Vault. The number of stickers and their arrangement vary by series.

Anyone can buy Wild, including people who do not hold the project's NFT. Holders of the relevant NFT can also buy the Wild stickers made for their own NFT number.

Packs include an element of surprise: some may contain special sticker variants alongside the regular stickers. This does not mean that every pack contains a special variant.

## Special stickers

Special stickers are made for particular events outside the regular series and are released through special series.

They have no fixed format. An event may use Vault, Wild, or both, with availability and purchasing conditions set for that event.
