Our main sticker types are **Vault**, **Wild**, and **Basic**. Their availability and pack contents depend on the project and series.

| Type | Personalization | Packaging | Who can buy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Vault** | Personalized, with a wallet address or registered wallet name | A sealed card case | Eligible snapshot holders, during the sales period |
| **Wild** | Personalized | A sticker pack without engraving or a storage case | Current NFT holders, within the remaining purchase limit |
| **Basic** | Not personalized | A sticker pack | Also available to people outside the community, when offered |

See [Purchasing & eligibility](/buying-and-eligibility) for the full rules.

## Vault

Vault is a limited-edition, personalized sticker made for collecting.

It is sold in a PS card case sealed by ultrasonic welding. An encrypted authentication chip identifies the individual item, making it possible to verify the Vault sticker as a unique piece.

At purchase, the buyer's blockchain wallet address or a name registered through a wallet naming service is used for personalization. The product also includes a specially designed background and identifying banner. Their form and arrangement may change between series.

A tradable **Vault NFT** is also issued when a Vault sticker is purchased.

## Wild

Wild is a personalized sticker made for everyday use.

It comes in a pack, without the engraving or storage case included with Vault. The number of stickers and their arrangement vary by series.

Packs include an element of surprise: some may contain special sticker variants alongside the regular stickers. This does not mean that every pack contains a special variant.

## Basic

Basic is not personalized. It is made to introduce a project or community to more people.

People who do not hold the relevant NFT or belong to the builder community can also buy Basic stickers. Basic is optional and may not be offered in every series.

## Special series

Special series are made for particular events outside the regular release schedule.

They have no fixed format. An event may use Vault, Wild, Basic, or a combination of these, with availability and purchasing conditions set for that event.
