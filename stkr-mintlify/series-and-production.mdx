We plan to release regular series each month, with special series for selected events.

Every regular series includes **Vault and Wild**. Basic may also be offered. Special series follow the format of the individual event.

## Made after an order

For regular series, production begins after an order is placed.

### Vault

1. The designer prepares the personalized sticker, background, and banner to match the series concept, including the buyer's wallet address or registered name.
2. Once the printed materials arrive, they are assembled with an encrypted authentication chip and a card case.
3. The case is sealed by ultrasonic welding.

### Wild

1. The designer prepares personalized stickers to match the series concept.
2. Special sticker variants are also produced, with the quantity based on the production run.
3. The finished stickers are packaged into Wild packs.

Pack contents vary. Producing special variants does not mean every pack contains one.

### Basic

1. The designer prepares non-personalized stickers to match the series concept.
2. The finished stickers are packaged into Basic packs.

### Special series

Production depends on the event. Some special-series stickers may be made before orders open.
