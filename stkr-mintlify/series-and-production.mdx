We plan to release regular series each month, with special or event series for selected events.

Every regular series includes **Vault and Wild**. Special and event series follow the format of the individual event.

## Production process

A regular series is announced the month before. When its month arrives, we reveal the series' key visual, created from the project's IP, and the stickers are made from that visual.

### Vault

1. The designer prepares the personalized sticker, background, and banner to match the series' key visual, including the buyer's wallet address or registered name.
2. Once the printed stickers arrive, they are finished in a card case together with an authentication chip.

### Wild

1. The designer prepares stickers to match the series' key visual.
2. Special sticker variants are also produced, with the quantity based on the production run.
3. The finished stickers are packaged into Wild packs.

Pack contents vary. Producing special variants does not mean every pack contains one.

### Special stickers

Production depends on the event.
