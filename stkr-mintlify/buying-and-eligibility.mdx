---
title: "Purchasing & eligibility"
description: "Vault needs the snapshot and current ownership. Wild is open to everyone."
---

Stickers are sold only through the **.STKR app for the relevant project**.

Users sign in with a wallet. Vault requires a wallet that proves a connection to the community or builder project, such as a wallet holding the relevant NFT. Wild is open to everyone, and NFT holders can also buy the Wild for their own NFT number. The rules below describe NFT-based eligibility.

## Vault: the snapshot and current ownership

A snapshot is a record of NFT ownership taken before a series is released.

Vault requires **both** conditions for the same NFT number. You must have held that NFT at the series snapshot, and you must still hold it when you buy. The snapshot record itself never changes.

Selling the NFT after the snapshot ends Vault eligibility for that series. Acquiring the NFT for the first time after the snapshot does not create Vault eligibility for that series.

## Wild: open to everyone

Wild does not require the community NFT. Anyone who signs in to the app can buy Wild during the sales period, within the purchase limit.

Holders of the relevant NFT can also buy the Wild stickers made for their own NFT number. This depends on **holding the NFT at the time of purchase**, not on the snapshot. Someone who acquires the NFT after a snapshot can buy the Wild for that number if the purchase limit has not been reached. Someone who sells the NFT can no longer buy the Wild for that number.

## Two examples

For an October series:

| Situation | October Vault | October Wild |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The linked wallet held the NFT at the snapshot, then sold it | Cannot buy, because the NFT is no longer held | Can buy the Wild open to everyone; cannot buy the Wild for the sold NFT's number |
| The linked wallet acquired the NFT after the snapshot | Cannot buy, because it was not held at the snapshot | Can buy the Wild for that NFT's number, if the purchase limit has not been reached |

These examples concern the NFT in question. Both assume that the series is still on sale and that the applicable purchase allowance remains available.

## The sales period applies to everyone

**Once a series' sales period ends, it can no longer be purchased, even if the user was previously eligible.**

Being in a snapshot does not extend the sales period.

## Special stickers

Availability and purchasing conditions for special stickers depend on the event. Check the series information for the types offered, sales period, and purchase limits.
