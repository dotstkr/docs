---
title: "Revenue & buybacks"
---

All sales revenue can be checked on-chain.

## Operating costs

We plan to use project income for production, infrastructure, staffing, and other running costs.

We plan to disclose these operating costs transparently on-chain **each quarter**.

## Two allocations

| Purpose | Allocation |
| --- | --- |
| Holder share | **10% of sales revenue** |
| Buybacks of collaborating brands' tokens, NFTs, or shares | **10% of profit** |

The buyback allocation is based on profit rather than revenue. We plan to use it to buy back the tokens, NFTs, or shares of collaborating brands.

## After a buyback

If a .STKR community forms, we plan to establish a way for that community to decide how the repurchased assets are used.

The decision-making process has not yet been specified.
